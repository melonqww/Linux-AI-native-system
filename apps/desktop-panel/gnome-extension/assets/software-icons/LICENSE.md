# Application icons

The PNG files in this directory are the application icons displayed on their
respective [Snap Store](https://snapcraft.io/store) listings. They are bundled
so the catalog works without downloading images at runtime. The files are
third-party artwork and are not covered by this repository's MIT license.
Product names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.
